# Freshmen_Explr
Interactive exploration, zombie apocalypse adventure game.

Story line ---

In this little town....
Everything was perfect.

Until, an invasive species from the unknown spread into our land.

Those who preyed upon the species, were infected. No one knew. They couldn't find the source; x-ray scans revealed that there were no signs of external life left alive. Yet those beings seemed to shift. Minor mutations over time, unti they were unrecognizable. That's how it spread. The chain continued until it eventually reached humans. The humans who consumed the infection, walked unaware. They continued to spread the disease during gatherings, and soon the disease became air-born. 

And that's when it happened. The lockdown. The Apocalypse. 

Humans began turning to their primative ways. Violent. Bloodshed. Until only the those who remained were thinned out. We were spread across the land. My parents were severly ill. They haven't found a cure for the disease. My sister and I have no idea what to do. We quarentined them to the basement. There have been some speculation on a cure but until then, we don't know what to do. 

I plan to go out tommorrow. I have to find more rations for the month. 

I head to bed. Tomorrow will hopefully be good...

Story line 1 - You run away, your parents and your sister die.
Story line 2 - Your sister kills you in your sleep and herself
Story line 3 - You save you and your sister and live far away into the future
Story line 4 - Your entire family is now zombies and your mind essence fades to black
